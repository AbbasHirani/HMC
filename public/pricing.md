# Pricing & Service Terms — Hirani Marketing Combines

Hirani Marketing Combines operates a transparent quoting model for all equipment supply and repair services. Because industrial hardware and pump conditions vary significantly, we do not list flat repair rates. Instead, we adhere to the following structured pricing process.

## 1. Diagnostics & Inspection
- **Price:** Free initial visual inspection at our Parrys workshop.
- **Process:** We inspect the equipment (pumps, RO systems, compressors, etc.) to identify the fault.
- **Result:** We issue a formal, written quote detailing parts and labor *before* any work begins.

## 2. Equipment Repair & Reconditioning
- **Price:** Custom per-job based on the diagnostic quote.
- **Policy:** No work begins without client approval of the quote. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.
- **Parts Included:** Only genuine, branded replacement components are used (e.g., mechanical seals, bearings, impellers, windings).
- **Turnaround:** Standard jobs are completed within 48–72 hours after quote approval.

## 3. New Equipment Supply
- **Price:** Competitive market rates based on capacity and specification.
- **Consultation:** We match the equipment (monoblock, submersible, hydro test pump) to the exact application requirements to prevent over-specification or under-performance.
- **Brands:** We are an authorised dealer for Kent, Aquaguard, CRI, Kirloskar, Grundfos, Crompton, Texmo, Shakti, Wilo, and Pentair.

## 4. Payment Terms
- **Accepted Methods:** Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, Cash, and standard corporate payment terms for verified industrial clients.
- **Invoicing:** Full GST-compliant invoices provided for all sales and services.
